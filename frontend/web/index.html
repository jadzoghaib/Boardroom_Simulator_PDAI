📖 Game Instructions

🚀 Getting Started

This simulation puts you in the role of an AI startup founder. You'll make strategic decisions each quarter to grow your company from startup to success.

📋 Setup Phase

  • Select a classmate as your founder
  • Choose your co-founder type (Technical, Business, or Solo)
  • Pick your country and startup sector
  • Enter your (auto-filled) background information
  • Click "Generate Setup" to initialize your company

💰 Key Metrics

  • Cash: Money in the bank. You lose cash each quarter based on burn rate.
  • Runway: Quarters until you run out of cash. Must keep above 2 to stay alive.
  • Valuation: Your company's worth. Better for fundraising and exit.
  • Milestones: Major achievements. Complete 3 to win!
  • Burn Rate: How much cash you spend each quarter.

⚡ Attention Points (AP)

Each quarter you get a limited pool of AP (Attention Points). These represent your focus and decision-making power. You must allocate them wisely across:

  • Milestone progress (moving toward your goals)
  • Hiring (building your team)
  • Fundraising
  • Strategic pivots and decisions

👥 Team Management

Hiring people increases your AP pool (more hands = more work gets done) but also increases burn rate. Balance growth with financial sustainability.

🎯 Winning Strategy

  • Complete all 3 milestones before money runs out
  • Raise funding strategically to extend runway
  • Hire selectively to boost productivity without burning too much cash
  • Respond to market events and competitor moves
  • Make quarterly decisions in the boardroom

💡 Pro Tip: Most founders fail on their first attempt—that's by design! The game teaches through experience and replay. Don't worry about losing; learn from it and try again.
