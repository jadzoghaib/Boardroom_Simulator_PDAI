How to Play

ESADE Entrepreneurs — 8-Quarter Startup Simulation

You are the CEO of a startup.

Survive 8 quarters (2 years), grow your company, beat your rivals, and complete 3 milestones before time runs out.

📋
Decision Events
Each quarter you face 2–3 real business dilemmas. Choose wisely — your stats, cash, and reputation are on the line.
💬
Ask Your Advisors
Before deciding, chat with your Celebrity co-founder and Professor advisor. They give LLM-powered perspectives.
📈
8 Stats to Grow
Growth · Brand · Product · Tech · Ops · Finance · Innovation · Execution. Events move these stats up or down.
🏦
Board Reviews
Every 2 quarters, your VC board reviews your progress. Impress them → valuation +20%. Disappoint → -10%.

Each quarter follows this flow:

1
Events Phase
2–3 decision cards appear. Each has 3 choices costing 1–3 AP. You can ask advisors before choosing. Run out of AP → go to step 3.
2
Hire & Fundraise (Optional)
Before ending the quarter, you can hire an exec (adds burn but boosts stats + AP) or raise a funding round (injects cash, dilutes equity).
3
End Quarter
Click End Quarter. The engine deducts burn, adds revenue, simulates rival moves, shifts the market, and checks milestones. You see a summary.
4
Board Review (Q2, Q4, Q6, Q8)
Every 2 quarters the VC board reviews you. Have a real conversation — defend your strategy. The verdict changes your valuation.
Market Conditions change each quarter — Boom, Recession, AI Hype, Talent War, etc. They multiply your revenue and burn rate automatically.

⚡ AP = Action Points

AP is your decision budget each quarter. Think of it as the management attention your team has available.

🎓 Professor Advisor

Chat with them for a more structured, analytical take on the same decision.

⚠️ Their chat is advisory only — it does NOT affect game stats.

Key Metrics Explained
Cash — money in the bank. Hits zero → game over.
Burn Rate — monthly spending (salaries, ops). Goes up when you hire execs.
Revenue — monthly income. Grows through product events and good decisions.
Runway — months until cash runs out at current burn − revenue. Keep it above 6 months.
Valuation — your startup's worth. Grows with revenue; affected by board reviews and market conditions.
Equity Given — % of company you've sold. Lower is better. Crosses 30% → investor risk zone.
Funding Stages
BootstrapStarting point — no external investors
AngelsSmall cheques, ~5–8% equity
SeedBigger round, product-market fit needed
Series AInstitutional VC, proven growth
Series BScale-up capital — final stage in game
💡 Strategy: Don't raise too early (you'll dilute at a low valuation). Don't raise too late (you'll run out of cash). Watch your runway — raise when you have 6+ months left.
🏆 How to Win

Complete all 3 sector milestones by the end of Q8. Each milestone requires your stats to hit certain thresholds.

You also need to survive — if cash hits 0 at any quarter end, it's game over regardless of stats.

🏁 Milestones

3 milestones are set at the start based on your sector (e.g. AI: Model-Market Fit, Enterprise Pipeline, AI Platform Dominance).

Each requires a combination of high stats (e.g. Tech + Product ≥ 25, or Growth + Brand ≥ 30).

Check the Milestones panel on the right to track progress.

⚔️ Rivals

3 rival startups compete in your sector. They gain 1–3 points per quarter automatically.

If a rival reaches a milestone before you, you lose the advantage — and potential valuation.

Watch the Rivals panel to track their momentum (Rising / Steady / Falling).

💀 Game Over Conditions

• Cash runs out at the end of any quarter → Bankruptcy

• ML score drops below 30% from Q3 onwards → Investors pull out

• Q8 ends → final score based on valuation, milestones, and ML probability
